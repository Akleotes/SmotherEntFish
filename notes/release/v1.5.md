Smotherfish 1.5: a Stockfish-strength chess engine that plays for the smothered mate.

## What's new

- **More true smothered mates.** Once a game is decided, the engine now
  holds out for a true smother for 80 construction moves (was 40) before it
  also accepts a near one ("all but one" square blocked), and may build for
  up to 160 moves (was 80). The king's box is scored in steps by how many
  squares next to it are still open.
- **Results at 3 minutes + 2 seconds** (smothers per 100 games, against 1.4.1):

  | Opponent | True: 1.4.1 | True: 1.5 | True or near: 1.4.1 | True or near: 1.5 |
  | -------- | ----------- | --------- | ------------------- | ----------------- |
  | Stockfish, Elo 1500 | 12.3 | 13.2 | 70.4 | 63.6 |
  | Stockfish, Elo 2500 | 6.9 | 8.8 | 58.3 | 51.8 |
  | Maia-3 1500 (human-like) | 8.8 | 17.3 | 66.8 | 62.3 |

  At 5+0.05, true smothers roughly double against Stockfish Elo 1500-2500.
- **No strength cost:** -1.0 Elo against 1.4.1 over 18,000 games at 10+0.1
  (95% -2.2 to +0.1).
- Human opponents often resign lost positions before mate; for the old,
  quicker behaviour set `SmotherPartialAfter 40` and `SmotherBuildMoves 80`.
- `HumanElo` (from 1.4.1) is unchanged.
