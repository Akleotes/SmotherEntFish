Smotherfish 1.4.1: a Stockfish-strength chess engine that plays for the smothered mate.

## What's new

- **`HumanElo` recalibrated.** Measured directly against the human-like
  Maia-3 network (300 games per point): in 1.4, `HumanElo 1350` played
  like about 1490 and `HumanElo 2000` like about 2400. Now 1350 plays like
  about 1350, 1600 like 1595, 1800 like 1805 and 2200 like 2115. The range
  is 1020 to 2400 (Maia-3's top levels are too compressed to measure
  above that).
- Full-strength play and smother hunting are unchanged from 1.4 (bench
  1210779). New experimental build-score options are off by default.
