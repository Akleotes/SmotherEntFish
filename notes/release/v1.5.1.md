Smotherfish 1.5.1: a Stockfish-strength chess engine that plays for the smothered mate.

## What's new

- **Smarter against people.** Human players resign lost games: on Lichess
  (33 million games measured), only about a quarter of clearly lost blitz
  games reach mate. When the GUI says the opponent is human (the standard
  `UCI_Opponent` option, sent by lichess-bot), Smotherfish now accepts a
  near smother after 40 construction moves instead of 80. Against an
  opponent that resigns like a real Lichess player, that kept every true
  smother (5.4 per 100 games) and doubled the total (17.5 vs 8.5).
- Against computers, in analysis and in GUIs that do not send opponent
  information (such as DroidFish), nothing changes from 1.5.
- `SmotherHumanAfter` sets the human switch point (0 = off).
- No change to playing strength (bench 1210779).
