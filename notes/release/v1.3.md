Smotherfish 1.3: a Stockfish-strength chess engine that plays for the smothered mate.

## What's new

- **Construction weights tuned on game results.** Games ending in a full or
  near smother at 3 minutes + 2 seconds: 71.6 per 100 vs Elo 1500 (was 59.8),
  65.8 vs Elo 2000 (62.0), 60.9 vs Elo 2500 (49.2).
- **No strength cost:** +1.3 Elo vs 1.2 over 21,600 games at 10+0.1
  (95% +0.2 to +2.4). 1.2 measured equal to unmodified Stockfish
  (-0.3 Elo over 43,200 games).
- **Human mode** (`HumanBlunder`): with Skill Level 0, `HumanBlunder 1` plays
  like a human of about 1330-1350 (measured against the human-like Maia-3
  network); 2 for about 1300, 3-5 for weaker. Off by default.
